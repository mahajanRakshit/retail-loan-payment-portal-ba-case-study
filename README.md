# Retail Loan & Payment Portal Enhancement
## Business Analyst Portfolio Case Study

**Project type:** Self-initiated academic / portfolio simulation  
**Role:** Business Analyst (Student Portfolio Project)  
**Methodology:** Agile Scrum  
**Tools demonstrated:** Jira (backlog simulation), Confluence (documentation structure), draw.io (process diagrams), Excel/Google Sheets (traceability and test cases), GitHub (version control)

> **Portfolio disclosure:** This is a fictional banking scenario created for learning and interview demonstration. It is not work delivered for, approved by, or affiliated with a real bank. The requirements, workflows, sample data and outcomes are illustrative.

## 1. Business problem
A fictional retail bank's existing portal creates friction for customers applying for personal loans and making repayments. Customers report unclear application status, repeated data entry, incomplete document submissions, slow eligibility checks and limited visibility into EMI payments.

## 2. Proposed solution
Enhance the portal with:
- Guided customer onboarding and profile capture
- Document checklist and upload status
- Preliminary loan eligibility verification
- Application reference number and status tracking
- EMI schedule and payment initiation
- Payment receipt and transaction history
- Exception handling for failed or pending payments
- Notifications for important application and payment events

## 3. Project objectives (illustrative targets)
- Reduce incomplete loan applications by 25%.
- Reduce avoidable customer follow-up calls by 20%.
- Make application status visible at each major stage.
- Provide customers with a clear EMI schedule and payment history.
- Improve traceability of requirements and UAT coverage.

These are proposed targets for the case study, not measured results.

## 4. Scope
### In scope
Customer registration, profile capture, document upload, preliminary eligibility checks, application tracking, EMI schedule display, payment initiation, payment status, receipts, notifications and basic operational exception handling.

### Out of scope
Actual credit underwriting, credit bureau integration, real money movement, core banking integration, production security certification, collections, loan disbursement and legal/compliance approval. These would require bank-specific discovery and specialist review.

## 8. How to present this in an interview
"I created a self-initiated Business Analyst case study based on a fictional retail banking portal. I started by defining the business problem and stakeholders, then documented business and functional requirements. I converted requirements into user stories with acceptance criteria, mapped the current and proposed processes, prioritized a sample Agile backlog, and prepared UAT test cases and a traceability matrix. The purpose was to practice the end-to-end BA lifecycle."

## 9. Important interview honesty
- Say "I created" or "I simulated" this case study.
- Do not say a real bank hired you or that a live portal was deployed.
- Do not claim actual JIRA/Confluence usage unless you create the project and pages in those tools. This repository includes a backlog and documentation structure you can import or recreate.
- Do not claim the illustrative targets were achieved. They are proposed success measures.
