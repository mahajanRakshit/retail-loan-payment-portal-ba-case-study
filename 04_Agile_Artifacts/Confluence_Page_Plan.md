# Suggested Confluence Space Structure

Create a space named **Retail Loan Portal – BA Case Study**.

1. Project Home
   - Project summary, objectives, scope, stakeholders and links.
2. Business Requirements
   - BRD, business rules, assumptions and open questions.
3. Functional Requirements
   - FRD, functional requirements, NFRs and exception handling.
4. Process Design
   - AS-IS, TO-BE, Context DFD and Level-1 DFD.
5. Agile Delivery
   - Epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, sprint plan and decision log.
6. Testing
   - UAT scenarios, test cases, defects and RTM.
7. Governance
   - RAID log, glossary, approvals and change history.

Use page version history and label pages consistently. Do not claim these pages exist in Confluence until you create them in your own workspace.
