# Project Charter

## Project title
Retail Loan & Payment Portal Enhancement

## Background
The fictional bank offers retail personal loans through branches and a digital portal. The current customer journey is fragmented: customers may not understand which documents are required, whether their application is complete, what stage it is in, or whether an EMI payment has succeeded.

## Problem statement
The current process lacks clear, consistent status visibility and guided customer interactions. This can lead to incomplete submissions, repeat contacts with support and uncertainty around repayments.

## Business objective
Design a clearer digital journey for loan application and repayment, with transparent status updates, document tracking and payment confirmation.

## Proposed success measures
| Measure | Illustrative target |
|---|---:|
| Reduction in incomplete applications | 25% |
| Reduction in avoidable status enquiries | 20% |
| Applications with visible stage/status | 100% |
| Successful payment attempts with receipt/reference | 100% |

Targets are hypothetical and would need baseline data and stakeholder approval.

## Assumptions
- The bank has an existing customer portal.
- A loan operations team reviews submitted applications.
- A payment service provider would be integrated by the bank's technology team.
- Eligibility shown in the portal is preliminary and does not constitute final loan approval.
- Customer identity, consent, retention and security requirements will be validated by compliance and security teams.

## Constraints
- No live banking, credit bureau or payment integrations are included in this portfolio simulation.
- No real customer data is used.
- Regulatory and bank-specific policy requirements are outside the scope of this learning case study.

## High-level milestones
1. Discovery and stakeholder mapping
2. AS-IS analysis
3. Requirements elicitation and BRD
4. FRD and process design
5. Backlog refinement and sprint planning
6. UAT preparation and traceability
7. Final review and portfolio publication
